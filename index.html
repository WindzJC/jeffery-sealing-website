About Jeffery Sealing

Experience behind the fiction.

Jeffery Sealing is a Colorado author whose fiction draws on a broad range of real-world experience.

He served eight years in the United States Navy as a Damage Controlman Second Class Petty Officer. He later earned associate's and bachelor's degrees in Criminal Justice, completed police academy training and became a certified private investigator.

His professional background has also included adult corrections, juvenile corrections, armed security and unarmed security.

Jeffery also publishes under the pen name Girad Clacy, where his catalogue expands into science fiction, espionage, military thrillers and action-adventure.
